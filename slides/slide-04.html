The mindset shift

Real attacks are chains, not bugs

Every link is something a developer waved away as “that never works.” Stack enough of them and you get a very bad afternoon.

“CSS can't make a network request.” — background:url() can.

“It can't read data.” — attribute selectors can, one character at a time.

“The sanitizer strips that.” — it strips a different copy than the browser runs.

“CSP blocks it.” — not if the exfil needs no external request.
